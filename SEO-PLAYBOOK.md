# Horizon Vantage - search and AI visibility playbook

What the site now does for you is in code (structured data, titles, FAQ, llms.txt).
Most of the remaining gain is off-site and only the owner can do it. In priority order:

## 1. Fix the Google Business Profile (biggest single lever)
Local searches ("photographer Wicklow") are won by the profile, not the website.
- **Primary category**: currently "Marketing agency". Change it to **Photographer**
  (or Commercial photographer). Add secondary categories: **Video production service**,
  **Web designer**, **Internet marketing service**.
- **Hours**: it shows "Open 24 hours". Set real hours (or "by appointment").
- **Service area**: add Wicklow, Kildare, Wexford (and Dublin if you travel). If you work from
  home, hide the street address and run it as a service-area business.
- **Services and products**: list each service (business photography, videography, website design,
  Google profile setup) with a one-line description and "from" price.
- **Description**: plain and specific - what you do, who for, where. Do not add keywords to the
  business *name*; Google suspends profiles for that.
- **Photos**: upload 10+ real shots from the gallery, then a few each month. Post an update weekly.
- **Website link**: https://horizonvantage.ie/ (not a tracking URL).
- **Reviews**: you have 2. Ask every client (Irish Exotic Pets, Furry Butts, Frascati Car Wash,
  past shoots) - send them the review link, reply to every review. Reviews are the strongest local
  ranking factor and the main thing AI assistants quote.

## 2. Get the site indexed (a new domain has no traction until Google knows it)
- **Google Search Console**: add `horizonvantage.ie` as a Domain property, submit
  `https://horizonvantage.ie/sitemap-index.xml`, then "Request indexing" for the homepage and the
  four service pages. Check Pages > "Crawled - currently not indexed".
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import the site from Search Console. ChatGPT search and Copilot use
  Bing's index, so this is how you show up there.
- Re-check in 2 weeks and 6 weeks. New domains typically take 2-6 months to rank for anything
  competitive.

## 3. Backlinks and mentions (what AI assistants and Google trust)
- Ask the clients whose sites you built (irishexoticpets.ie, furrybutts.com) to add a footer line:
  `Website by <a href="https://horizonvantage.ie/">Horizon Vantage</a> - web design in Co. Wicklow`.
  Descriptive links from real client sites are the cheapest strong signal you can get.
- Free listings with identical name, phone and website everywhere: Facebook (done), Bing Places,
  Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn company page, Golden Pages, Wicklow Chamber / Local Enterprise Office
  directories, any Irish photographer or web-designer directories.
- Put https://horizonvantage.ie/ in your Instagram and Facebook bios and in your email signature.

## 4. Content that matches how people search
- One case-study page per finished client (what the problem was, what you shot/built, a screenshot,
  a quote if they will give one). Send me the details for Furry Butts and Irish Exotic Pets and I will
  build them.
- Short guides that answer questions business owners type: "How to set up a Google Business Profile",
  "How much does a business website cost in Ireland", "What photos should a holiday let have".

## 5. About AI recommendations
No one can guarantee an AI assistant recommends a business. What raises the odds: the same facts about
you on many independent sites (profile, directories, client footers), real reviews, clear pricing, and
pages that answer the question directly. The site now has all of those on its side
(structured data, FAQ answers, `llms.txt`, AI crawlers allowed in `robots.txt`); items 1-3 supply
the rest.
