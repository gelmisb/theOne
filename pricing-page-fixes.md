# Horizon Vantage: Pricing Page Fixes

**Decision:** Keep prices public. Hiding them would most likely cost you more good enquiries than it saves. The problem is not that prices are shown. The problem is how the page is structured: too many tiers, a low first anchor, and bare "from" prices.

Work top to bottom. P1 fixes the structure, P2 the messaging, P3 compliance, P4 trust, P5 is a pricing decision, and the last section is measurement.

---

## P1: Structure (highest impact)

### 1. Cut six tiers down to three core cards
Currently six tiers of three different kinds (recurring, small one-off, website builds, flagship) compete for attention. Owners who don't know what they need struggle to choose, and the two website tiers are hard to tell apart (neither includes a shoot).

- [ ] Merge **Starter Website (€900)** and **Website Only (€1,400)** into one **Website** card: from €900, "most clients spend €1,100-€1,600", with a template or custom option inside it.
- [ ] Keep **Booking-Ready Launch** as the highlighted card (€2,400-€3,200, typical ~€2,800). Keep the "Core offer" badge.
- [ ] Add a third premium card as an anchor, e.g. **Season Partner**: Launch + 12 months of Care & Visibility + two Season Refreshes, from around €4,500. Only add it if you are genuinely willing to sell and deliver it. Its job is to make the Launch look like the safe middle option.
- [ ] If you don't want a third card, put the **Launch first** and the Website card second. Showing the higher price first nudges people towards higher-priced options.

### 2. Move recurring and small items out of the main row
Right now the first number a visitor sees is €120/month, and a €150 one-off sits beside the €2,400 flagship. That anchors everyone low and makes the flagship look 16 times the "cheapest option".

- [ ] Add a section below the main row: **"Keep it working"** containing Care & Visibility (from €120/month). Also show "≈ €1,440/yr" and what one extra booking a month is worth to a B&B or restaurant.
- [ ] Add a section below that: **"Quick wins & add-ons"** containing Local SEO Help Only (from €150), Season Refresh (from €450) and the NFC card (€25-35).

### 3. Replace bare "from" prices with typical spend
A bare "from €900" becomes the price people expect to pay, and a higher quote after the audit then feels like a bait-and-switch.

- [ ] Add a "Most clients spend €X-€Y" line to **every** card, not only the Launch.
- [ ] Use one typical figure on the Launch (e.g. "typically ~€2,800"). A single typical number tends to work better than a wide range alone.

---

## P2: Messaging

### 4. Rewrite the audit call to action
"Free audit" can read as "sales call". It is currently the only route to a firm number.

- [ ] Say what the audit actually is, e.g. "15-minute call or a written review of your Google profile and site. No obligation."
- [ ] State when they get the quote, e.g. "Written quote within 48 hours."
- [ ] Offer two buttons: **Book a free audit** and **Get a ballpark in 24 hours**. Only add the second if you can honour the turnaround.

### 5. Add a "What changes the price" list
- [ ] List the main variables near the pricing: page count, booking or ordering system, travel outside your area, extra shoot time, extra video cuts.

### 6. Make the Trading Online Voucher visible on the Launch card
This can be a real selling point. It covers up to €2,500 or 50% of eligible spend (excluding VAT), whichever is lower, for businesses with up to 10 employees that have been trading at least six months.

- [ ] Add a line to the Launch card: "May be eligible for the Trading Online Voucher (up to 50%)". Keep the wording as "may be eligible".
- [ ] Add a short explainer (eligibility, approval must come **before** any cost is incurred, how to apply through the Local Enterprise Office).
- [ ] Check with your LEO whether the photography and video parts count as eligible spend before promising anything. This varies by office.

### 7. State your VAT position
- [ ] Add "Prices shown, no VAT charged" if you are not VAT registered, or "Prices exclude VAT" if you are. Confirm the current services threshold and what applies to you with your accountant.

### 8. Add the small trust details
- [ ] "Prices valid for 2026" note, and review the prices each season so you aren't locked in.
- [ ] Payment terms (deposit, stage payments, when the balance is due).
- [ ] Keep "Final quote after your free audit" under every card. It is the right hybrid and the page already does this well.

---

## P3: Compliance

### 9. Fix the NFC "Tap to Review" card copy
Google's review policy does not allow selectively soliciting positive reviews, offering incentives, or pressuring people to leave reviews on the premises. A misworded product page could put your clients' Google profiles at risk.

- [ ] Remove or avoid wording like "get more 5-star reviews" or any suggestion of handing the card only to happy guests.
- [ ] Use neutral wording, e.g. "Make it easy for every guest to leave an honest review."
- [ ] Update the line "bundled into Tier 1/3, or sold on its own". The tier numbers will no longer match after the restructure. Better: make it a free bonus with any Launch, and a €25-35 add-on otherwise.
- [ ] Move it out of its tier-sized banner and into the add-ons section.

---

## P4: Trust

### 10. Put proof directly above the prices
- [ ] Add one or two hospitality case studies with real results (enquiries, bookings, search ranking) immediately above the pricing section.
- [ ] If you don't have client results yet, use your own pilot work and label it clearly as such. Don't use invented testimonials or numbers.

---

## P5: Decide on price levels

### 11. Check whether the flagship is underpriced
At €2,400, the Launch includes a half-day shoot, a property film plus 3 cuts, a 5-8 page booking site and Google Business Profile work. Irish agency guides put a small-business website alone at roughly €1,500-€6,500, and some put the starting point at €2,500+. Those figures come from agencies' own marketing pages, so treat them as rough benchmarks. The risk of publishing is more likely "too cheap" than "too expensive".

- [ ] Consider raising the Launch by 10-20% (e.g. €2,700-€3,500), especially if the voucher is shown.
- [ ] Sense-check that the price is sustainable for one person's time across shoot, edit, build and the 30-day check-in.

---

## Measurement (3-6 months)

Your traffic is probably too low for proper A/B testing, so track simple signals.

- [ ] Add two questions to the enquiry form: **"Roughly what budget do you have in mind?"** and **"Did you see our pricing?"**
- [ ] Record for each enquiry: source, budget fit, whether it became a quote, whether it closed.
- [ ] Compare enquiry volume, budget fit and close rate before and after the restructure. Review after 3 months, then again at 6.

---

## Decisions for you before you start

1. Do you want a premium third tier, or will you lead with the Launch instead?
2. Are you VAT registered, and how should prices be displayed?
3. Can you honour a 24-hour ballpark and a 48-hour written quote?
4. Do you want to test a higher Launch price now or after you have case studies?
5. Do you have real client results to show yet?
